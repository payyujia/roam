Roam design system · v3

Teal leads. Pink accents.

One brand colour carries the whole app. Selection and progress inside any one component are shades of that same teal, never a second hue. Pink marks the social and the soon: friends, ratings, due-soon. Flat fills, no borders, no highlights. Tap anything: it’s live.

01 · Colour

Teal gets seven steps so a single component can show state without borrowing another hue. Pink gets four. Red exists only for errors. Green, yellow and violet are gone.

05 · Rows, cards & lists

Due-date tiers keep their four repo classes (.tier.t0–t3) but now mean something: t1 urgent (pink mid), t2 soon (pink tint), t3 later (teal tint), t0 past (neutral). Red is reserved for errors. Covers and avatars use four colours (teal, pink, neutral) instead of ten. Photos are replaced by colour blocks here because published pages can’t load remote images; once real photos land, .cover shows the photo and the tier tint becomes the only colour on the row. The .l-* colours stay as the loading fallback.
